Get in touch
How do I request a ride?

Enter your pick-up, destination, date and time, then add your contact details. Send the prepared request via WhatsApp or email. Your ride is arranged only after personal confirmation.

Can I arrange my ride in advance?

Yes. Let us know your preferred date and time. We will check the arrangements and confirm the details with you personally.

How much will my ride cost?

Applicable taxi tariffs apply to arranged taxi rides. Individual private-hire journeys are quoted and agreed with the operator in advance. Our fares page provides an overview.

Who operates the ride?

MEY Taxi München arranges rides with independent, licensed taxi and private-hire operators. We do not operate our own fleet. Your transport contract is with the operator carrying out the ride.

Where would you like to go?

Tell us your destination. We’ll help arrange your ride.
